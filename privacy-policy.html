Privacy Policy

Wecreate - Photo Video Maker (Android, package com.tiklatestvideos)
Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Wecreate - Photo Video Maker app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is published by Dreams Way apps. If you have any questions, contact us at dreamswayapp@gmail.com.

In short
  • You don't need an account. We don't run servers that receive your photos, music, videos, name or messages.
  • Your photos and music stay on your phone. Videos are made on your device and saved to your gallery.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and related data to serve and measure ads, as described below.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in the app.

1. Information you provide inside the app

Photos

You choose photos with Android's photo picker. The app only gets access to the photos you select, not to your whole gallery. Copies of the selected photos are stored in the app's private storage so you can keep editing your video. They are never uploaded to us.

Music

If you pick your own song, a copy is stored in the app's private storage so it can be added to your video. It is not uploaded to us.

Text you type

Names and wishes you type into a template are saved on your device as part of your draft and your saved videos. They are not sent to us.

Background removal

The “remove background” feature uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your device. Your photos are not sent to us or to Google to do this. ML Kit may send Google basic, non-content technical information (such as device model, app version and performance metrics) to keep the feature working and improve it. See ML Kit terms.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Drafts and saved videos: your current draft, a list of the videos you've made, and the photo and music copies they use.
  • Finished videos: saved to your device's gallery (the Movies folder), where other apps you allow can see them.
  • Settings: your chosen language, how many videos you've made (used to space out ads), and which premium templates you've unlocked and until when.

This information stays on your device. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which Google stores under your account according to your backup settings. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and Switzerland). The app shows banner, native, interstitial, rewarded and app-open ads.

To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device or app identifiers.
  • IP address, from which an approximate (not precise) location may be inferred.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, app version and network type.
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped.
  • Diagnostics, such as ad-related crash and performance information.

Depending on your consent and settings, these ads may be personalised based on your interests. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Rewarded ads

Some features (unlocking premium templates for 24 hours, removing the watermark, or saving in HD) are offered in exchange for watching a rewarded ad. These options are clearly marked “Ad”, and watching them is always your choice.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): when you first open the app, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the app via Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Google ad settings: manage ad personalisation for your Google account at adssettings.google.com.

Opting out of personalised ads doesn't remove ads; you'll see ads that are less relevant to you.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't share your photos, music, videos or text with anyone. Information is shared only:

  • With Google, for advertising and the services described above.
  • When you choose to share a video, for example to WhatsApp, Instagram or YouTube. The video goes to that app, and its own privacy policy applies.
  • If required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Saved videos: delete one by long-pressing it in My Videos. This also removes the copy in your gallery.
  • Everything else: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all app data on your device.
  • Gallery copies: videos you've shared or copied elsewhere must be deleted where they are stored.
  • Data held by Google for ads: this is kept according to Google's retention policy.

Because we don't hold your data on our servers, we have nothing to delete on our side. If you have a request or question, email us and we'll help.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or state privacy laws in the United States), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. For data in the app, you can use the controls described above. For data processed by Google for ads, use the ad choices in section 4 or contact Google. You can also email us at dreamswayapp@gmail.com, or complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

Your photos, music and drafts are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but because your content never leaves your device through us, the risk is kept to a minimum.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes or the law requires it. We'll change the effective date at the top, and for significant changes we'll let you know in the app or on its Google Play page.
